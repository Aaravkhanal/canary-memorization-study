# 48M scale-transfer experiment protocol

**Status: the 14M queue is complete. Eleven of 24 48M cells are complete and verified: all six baseline frequencies,
plus learned-position frequencies 0, 1, 10, 50, and 100.** Frequency 0 required an initial
step-1,500 checkpoint-write failure and an in-place resume from its verified step-1,000 checkpoint; it reached
best validation loss 1.4871 (PPL 4.4243). Frequency 1 reached best validation loss 1.4931 (PPL 4.45). Baseline
frequency 10 reached best validation loss 1.4896 (PPL 4.44), and frequency 50 reached 1.5018 (PPL 4.49). Baseline
frequencies 100 and 500 reached 1.5061 and 1.4802. Learned-position frequencies 0, 1, 10, 50, and 100 reached
1.5309, 1.5275, 1.5305, 1.5464, and 1.5472. Learned-position frequency 500 is currently training; the other 12 cells are queued. This is a scale-transfer extension to the exploratory
14M study, not a replacement for the contamination-free confirmatory study.

## Why the existing 48M checkpoint is not the experiment

MY-LLM's existing 48.1M checkpoint trained on a larger corpus for 15,000 steps and 245.76M tokens. The canary
study's 14.2M runs use its own frequency-specific corpora, 6,000 updates, 8,192 tokens/update, and 49.152M tokens.
Evaluating the existing checkpoint can provide a descriptive zero-insertion reference, but it cannot estimate
what controlled canary repetition does to the 48M model. The scale extension therefore trains fresh 48M models
inside this research repository on the exact same frequency-specific data and tokenizer used by the 14M arm.

## Registered scale arm

- Four architectures: 48.100M baseline, 48.428M learned-position, 47.239M MHA with nine layers, and 48.100M
  LayerNorm parameters.
- Six corpus conditions: 0, 1, 10, 50, 100, and 500 insertions.
- One exploratory training seed initially; this yields 24 independent from-scratch model runs. Seed replications
  are required before strong claims about the size interaction.
- Same data split, 4096-token tokenizer, 512-token context, microbatch 1, gradient accumulation 16, and 6,000
  optimizer updates as the 14M arm: 49,152,000 tokens per run. The separate MY-LLM checkpoint used
  245,760,000 tokens and is not a matched comparator.
- Learning rate is fixed at 5e-4 for all 48M conditions, following the existing large-model recipe. Since the 14M
  exploratory arm used 8e-4, the cross-scale comparison remains partly confounded by learning-rate choice; report
  this limitation and use a same-schedule replay of selected 14M cells if the initial scale signal merits
  confirmation.
- Memorization, argmax extraction, novelty generation, human annotation, and full-corpus overlap use the same
  evaluation code and definitions as the smaller arm. All model artifacts and result files stay in this research
  repository.

## Execution and interpretation

Configs are generated by `scripts/make_48m_configs.py`; the sequential runner is
`scripts/run_48m_scale_experiments.sh`. It waited for the 14M MPS queue and runs one model at a time.
Estimated compute is on the order of 150--200 laptop-hours for the 24 training jobs, based on prior throughput.
The first attempt ran from 02:14:47 to 04:17:06 local (2h02m19s wall time), reached 1,500 updates
(12,288,000 tokens), and failed during checkpoint writing with a PyTorch `unexpected pos` serialization error.
Its intact step-1,000 training checkpoint loaded and was verified. After approval to reclaim completed 14M rolling
checkpoints, this same cell resumed in-place at 07:36:09 and completed at 13:44:45 local on 2026-10-08. Its
summary reports 26,976.3 seconds of resumed training time, four gradient-norm anomalies, best validation loss
1.4871208 / PPL 4.4243. Memorization and novelty JSONs were generated; exact corpus overlap was computed against
the matching 73,139-story frequency-0 corpus. All retained checkpoints and JSON results were verified, then only
that cell's rolling checkpoints were removed, leaving best/final checkpoints, logs, and results. Baseline
frequency 1 then completed 6,000 updates (26,015.6 trainer seconds; 7h13m36s), best validation loss 1.4931227 /
PPL 4.45, with six gradient-norm spikes and seven gradient-health alerts. Both model checkpoints and all three
result JSONs were checked; its overlap output matches the frequency-1 corpus fingerprint and 73,139 stories.
Baseline frequency 10 started at 00:12:20 local on 2026-10-09 and completed at 07:57:05 (7h44m45s wall time;
27,860.3 trainer seconds). It completed 6,000 updates with best validation loss 1.48957798 / PPL 4.44, four

after successful validation.
Actual per-cell elapsed times and failures are the record of truth. The config tests check token budget,
seed/data separation, and exact parameter counts. The full research suite checks surrounding analysis and
evaluation code.

Do not call the 48M extension confirmatory: it reuses the exploratory canary bank, includes known contaminated
items (excluded from clean aggregates), and starts with one seed. Before a paper claim about scale dependence,
complete blinded human labels, the full-corpus copying analysis, and seed replication. The confirmatory protocol
remains in `docs/CONFIRMATORY_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md`.
