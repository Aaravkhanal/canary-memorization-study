cleanest options are either (a) provide a writable external volume with at least 25 GiB free and set
`CONFIRMATORY_ARTIFACT_ROOT`, or (b) the owner deliberately relocates or archives enough old experiments while
preserving their checksums. The confirmatory runner validates the selected filesystem before starting and does not
delete historical checkpoints.
